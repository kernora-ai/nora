# See a factlet cited

Ask your IDE agent something your factbook covers — a rule, threshold, or reversed decision your team already documented.

A grounded turn cites the factlet (for example `f155`) with its trust basis:

- `execution-proof`
- `human-confirmed`
- `agent-asserted`

Click the citation to open the decision trail. Superseded factlets are filtered from grounding so the model does not repeat a reversed decision.
